Features

Everything you need

Built for speed, privacy, and ease of use across all devices.

Instant Number Search

Enter any phone number and get results in real-time. See who has it saved, under what name, and from which contact list.

Contact Upload

Sync your contacts directly from your phone with one tap. The more people contribute, the more powerful the network becomes.

Phone OTP Verification

Your account is secured with phone-based one-time passwords. No email required. Fast, simple, and reliable identity verification.

Reveal Identity

Pay a small number of coins to reveal the full name of who saved a number. You control how much detail you need.

Daily Free Searches

Every user gets 5 free searches every single day — no purchase required. Use coins for more when you need them.

Invite & Earn

Share your unique referral code with friends. Every time someone signs up using your code, you both get free coins as a reward.

Privacy First

All contact data is encrypted and stored securely. You can remove your phone number from the database at any time.

Flexible Coin Packages

Purchase coin bundles starting from as little as you need. No subscriptions — buy only what you use, when you use it.
