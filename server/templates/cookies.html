Last updated: March 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how NUMID uses cookies and similar technologies on our website (numidapp.co). By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

Our mobile application does not use browser cookies. This policy applies only to the NUMID website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to website owners. Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remaining on your device for a set period).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Session management: To maintain your login session on the web version of the app.
  • Performance cookies: To understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve it.
  • Preference cookies: To remember your settings and preferences (such as language selection).

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not share cookie data with third-party advertisers.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Our website may include features from third-party services that may set their own cookies. These include:

  • Google Fonts: Used to load fonts for the website. Google may set performance cookies.
  • Google Analytics: Used to analyse website traffic. Data is anonymised and aggregated.

We do not control third-party cookies. Please refer to the respective providers' privacy policies for more information.

5. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies that have been set
  • Block all cookies or cookies from specific websites
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser
